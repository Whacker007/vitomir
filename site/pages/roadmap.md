# What's next

Vitomir 1.0 is the original game, faithfully. It is also the first thing built on a larger platform, and the rest is
already under way. None of what follows has a date: it comes when it is ready.

## The original game, kept faithful

The 1.0 release keeps being looked after: fixes for what players find, and, soon, the original game's own mods, so
that a mod which changes the game's archives plays on Vitomir as it did on 1.1.

## A platform for games

Vitomir is a platform that game modules run on: *Redemption* is the first, the **Classic** module. The same platform is
being built to carry much more: very large levels with many characters, modern rendering, new models and animation, and
rules kept outside the engine, where they can be read and changed. Everything is switchable, singly or together, with
the faithful game always there.

## A reimagined campaign

The largest work in progress is a **reimagined campaign**: the story's world rebuilt from new, original art, played in
first or third person with free movement, or by clicking as before.

- **Prague, around 1141**: plausible and prosperous rather than strictly historical, built new from research, Judith
  Bridge and all.
- **New characters and items**: original models, armour and weapons, with natural human movement.
- **New rules** for combat, the disciplines and the powers, tested and tuned in the open.
