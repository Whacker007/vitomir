# Support

## First and foremost: buy the game

The best way to support Vitomir is to buy the game it plays. Every copy sold tells the people who hold *Redemption*
that it is still loved.

- *Vampire: The Masquerade – Redemption* on [GOG](https://www.gog.com/en/game/vampire_the_masquerade_redemption) or
  [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/559680).

And if you have never played it, *Redemption*'s younger sibling is a game beloved by most of us:

- *Vampire: The Masquerade – Bloodlines* on [GOG](https://www.gog.com/en/game/vampire_the_masquerade_bloodlines) or
  [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2600).

page. [Report a bug](https://github.com/Whacker007/vitomir/issues).
