# Support

## First and foremost: buy the game

The best way to support Vitomir is to buy the game it is based on. Every copy sold tells the people who hold *Redemption*
that it is still loved.

- *Vampire: The Masquerade – Redemption* on [GOG](https://www.gog.com/en/game/vampire_the_masquerade_redemption) or
  [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/559680).

And if you have never played it, *Redemption*'s younger sibling is a game beloved by most of us, and it ended up much
more popular and better known:

- *Vampire: The Masquerade – Bloodlines* on [GOG](https://www.gog.com/en/game/vampire_the_masquerade_bloodlines) or
  [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2600).

There's also a ton of books and tabletop role-playing material out there for the World of Darkness and *Vampire: The
Masquerade* in general. *Redemption* draws on the older rules of the third edition (Revised), which a fair few of us elder
entities tend to prefer, along with the later fourth (the 20th Anniversary Edition). The newest game, *Bloodlines 2*,
is based on the fifth edition, which has had mixed reactions. If you like this game and are new to all of it, go and see
what's out there; Onyx Path has [a guide for *Redemption* fans](https://theonyxpath.com/?p=6619).

## Report what you find

A good bug report is worth a great deal: what you did, what happened, and the details from the launcher's **About**
page. [Report a bug](https://github.com/Whacker007/vitomir/issues).
