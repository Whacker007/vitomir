## Why this licence

Vitomir is a fan-made project, and a love letter to the original game that so many of us remember and played for hours
and hours. It is free to people who own the game, and it should always stay so. An open-source licence would have let
anyone upload it elsewhere or sell it, as long as they passed the code along; a closed one would have kept people from
learning from it and building on it. This one sits between: the code is public, and anyone may study it, change it and
build on it, but nobody may sell it, scatter copies of it across other sites, or use it to spread the game's own files.
It is adapted from a standard noncommercial licence written by licensing lawyers, with Vitomir's own conditions added.
Because it forbids selling, Vitomir is "source-available" rather than "open source" in the Open Source Initiative's
sense.
