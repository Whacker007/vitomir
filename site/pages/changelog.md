# Changes

## 1.0.0

The first public release: the original game, faithfully, on a modern PC.

- The whole of *Redemption* at patch 1.1, the single-player story, multiplayer and the Storyteller.
- Widescreen at any resolution, or the original 4:3; Direct3D 12 or Vulkan.
- The Fixes (off by default), the quality-of-life switches (on by default) and the repairs (always on): see
  [Features](features.md).
- The launcher: Setup imports your Steam or GOG copy and your old saves; player modules; upscaled movies; your user
  folder anywhere, or portable.
- Network games over the internet, with the router opened by itself where it can, and streamer mode.
